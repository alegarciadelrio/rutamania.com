Rent requirements

  • Valid motorcycle/rider license for this type of bike
  • Minimum age: 21 years old
  • Full physical and mental abilities (subject to our determination)
  • Full rental payment required before starting the trip
  • $1,500 USD security deposit. It is returned via Wise, SWIFT, ACH or PayPal within 40 days of the return date. Any fines or damages during the rental period are deducted from the deposit. Fines in Uruguay are expensive: a speed or red-light camera fine can cost around $400 USD and may arrive with a delay.
Rental terms & conditions
  • Motorcycles are delivered with a 12 V outlet for GPS.
  • The rate is per day. Rentals are calculated on calendar days: pickup and return days count as full days regardless of the time.
  • 30% of the total rental value is required as a reservation. The full payment must be made before delivery.
  • Rates include 200 km per day. Each additional kilometer costs $5.
  • The client is responsible for traffic violations during the rental period.
  • The motorcycle can't be taken to another country unless expressly agreed.
  • If the client damages the motorcycle, they are not entitled to a replacement unit or a refund of the unused rental.
  • Clients are responsible for damages exceeding the contracted coverage in accidents they're involved in, including personal damages, damages to third parties and to the motorcycle.
  • Motorcycles must be locked (steering lock) when left unattended.
  • Only one passenger is allowed per motorcycle.
  • Helmet and reflective clothing are mandatory at all times.
  • We reserve the right to refuse a rental at our sole discretion.
