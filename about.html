Our story

We love to ride our motorbike and discover the unknown, that is why we enjoy work on adventures. Motorcycles and Nature are our passions; and we are committed to offer a high quality service with affordable prices. We are based in Florida, Uruguay, It is accesible by bus from the airport or we can offer you airport pickup for a stressless trip.

Why go out and travel?

Since a child, I was riding motorbikes. I remember in some moments my father was leaving the handlebar and I was taking the control of the motorcycle. Since that, I love bikes, and I love the way that I feel the nature. I am a true believer that this nature is the creation of god, and go out and see this beautiful complexity is a joy for me. For this reason, I decided create this business to encourage people to go out and see the world. Even if you don’t rent a motorcycle or take a tour from us, you can contact and visit me, just for a chat. Information about Uruguay and Argentina, ferry connections, beautiful places, roads, tracks, or culture.
