About us

Our story

We love to ride and discover the unknown. That's why we work on adventures.

Motorcycles and nature are our passions, and we are committed to offering a high quality service at affordable prices. We are based in Florida, Uruguay. It is accessible by bus from the airport, or we can offer you airport pickup for a stress-free trip.

Why go out and travel?

Since I was a child, I've been riding motorbikes. I remember moments when my father let go of the handlebar and I took control of the motorcycle. Since then, I love bikes, and I love the way I feel nature. I am a true believer that this nature is the creation of God, and going out to see this beautiful complexity is a joy for me.

For this reason, I decided to create this business: to encourage people to go out and see the world. Even if you don't rent a motorcycle or take a tour with us, you can contact and visit me, just for a chat. Information about Uruguay and Argentina, ferry connections, beautiful places, roads, tracks, or culture.
