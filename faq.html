General info

What is Rutamania Adventures?

Rutamania Adventures is a motorcycle rental agency in Uruguay.

Motorcycles and nature are our passions, and we are committed to offering a high quality service at affordable prices. We are based in Florida, Uruguay. It is accessible by bus from the airport, or we can offer you airport pickup for a stress-free trip.

How can I rent a motorcycle?

Just contact us to check availability. These are the rent requirements:

  • International driver license for this kind of bike
  • Over 21 years old
  • Full physical and mental abilities, at our sole determination
  • Rental paid before starting the trip
  • Security deposit for the bike (see each bike's page)
Do I need experience to ride the motorcycle routes in Uruguay?

Not necessarily, although it is recommended to have previous experience riding motorcycles and to be familiar with the country's traffic rules.

How is the rental calculated?

Motorcycle rentals are calculated on calendar days: both pickup and return days count as full days, regardless of the time. 30% of the total rental value is required as a reservation.

What is the best time of year to ride in Uruguay?

Spring to autumn is the best time to travel by motorcycle in Uruguay, as temperatures are pleasant and there are fewer tourists.

Do I need a special license to ride in Uruguay?

Yes, a valid motorcycle driver's license is required to ride the country's routes.

Where can I pick up the motorcycle?

You can pick up the motorcycle in Florida, or we can deliver it to other parts of Uruguay: Montevideo and Montevideo Airport ($140), Colonia or Punta del Este ($220). Use the quote calculator on any bike page to see the total.

About our motorcycles

Which models do you have?

Offroad 200: four-stroke, single cylinder 200cc, OHC, 5 speed. Front tyre 21″, rear tyre 18″. Weight capacity 150 kg (rider included). Fuel tank 9 L, consumption 34 km/L. Front and rear luggage rack. From $38 per day.

Zanella ZR200: single cylinder 200cc, OHC, 6 speed. Front tyre 19″, rear tyre 17″. Weight capacity 150 kg (rider included). Fuel tank 12 L, consumption 34 km/L. Front and rear luggage rack. From $42 per day.

BMW G650GS: four-stroke, single cylinder 650cc, DOHC, 5 speed. Front tyre 19″, rear tyre 17″. Weight capacity 200 kg (rider included). Fuel tank 14 L, consumption 27 km/L. Rear luggage rack, optional 23 L panniers. From $90 per day.

Is there a better price for long rentals?

Yes, we can arrange a better price for long rentals and fewer kilometers. Feel free to ask us.

Can I take the motorcycle out of Uruguay?

It is possible with a previous agreement. We provide the necessary documents to export the motorcycle. Charges apply: notary fees are 90 USD.
