Arequita hiking trail

Ride to Minas, then hike to the top of a granite giant.

10 km north of the city of Minas, Cerro Arequita appears as a granite giant: an elevation of volcanic origin, 230 meters high.

There's the Parador and Mirador restaurant, visits to the grotto and the possibility of climbing the hill. Very close by is the municipal campsite, with capacity for 3,000 people.

Notable walk

A walk through a forest of more than 700 centenary ombúes, the second largest group of ombúes in Uruguay after Rocha's, which has about 3,000 trees. It is a rare phenomenon, since the ombú is generally a species that grows alone, near Tapas, and is originally from South America. Along the same hill you can reach the top of Cerro Arequita.
