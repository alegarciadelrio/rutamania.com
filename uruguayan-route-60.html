Uruguayan Route 60

The "panorámica": short, winding and full of views.

Route 60, in the east of the country, joins the departments of Maldonado and Lavalleja. This panoramic route goes from Route 9 at Pan de Azúcar until it joins Route 12 in the department of Lavalleja, near the city of Minas.

Notable journey

Despite being relatively short, on this journey you can see some of the best views in the country and find various attractions: the Mining Park, tourist ventures related to horses, boutique wineries with accommodation and a wonderful landscape. We encourage you to discover it and travel the landscapes that surround it!

Nearby

There are a couple of alternative tracks from Route 60. One is "Cañadón de la Palma", a great place with 3 waterfalls and a lot of hiking tracks. From there you also have Route 81, a great dirt route that crosses the "sierras". Route 60 continues into Route 12 towards Pueblo Edén and Punta Ballena. All of them are part of the Uruguayan Discovery Trail.
