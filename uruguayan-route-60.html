URUGUAYAN ROUTE 60

Route 60, in the east region, joins the departments of Maldonado and Lavalleja. This panoramic route, goes from Route 9 in Pan de Sugar, until you join Route 12 in the department of Lavalleja, near the city of Mines.

Notable journey
On its journey, despite being relatively short, you can see some of the best views in the country and we find various attractions: the Mining Park, tourist ventures related to horses, boutique wineries with accommodation and a wonderful landscape. Encourage you to discover it and travel the landscapes that surround it!
